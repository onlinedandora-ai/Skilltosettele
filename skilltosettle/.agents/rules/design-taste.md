# Design Taste & Frontend Quality Rules

## 1. Anti-AI Slop Mandates
- **No Rainbow Text Gradients:** Never apply multi-color pink/purple/neon text gradients to headlines. Use authoritative midnight navy (`#090e17`, `#0f172a`), deep slate, or burnished metallic gold (`#b45309`, `#d97706`).
- **Hard Em-Dash Ban:** Do not use robotic em-dashes (`—`) in headlines, hero titles, or punchlines. Use typographic bullets (`·`), clean slashes (`/`), colons, or concise editorial sentences.
- **No Symmetrical Boring Cards:** Avoid 3 identical centered cards with generic icons. Inject layout variance: asymmetrical bento grids, split-screen proof, and varied vertical rhythm.
- **No Purpose-less Floating Blobs:** Background blurs must not look like random neon stains. Use subtle radial lighting and architectural mesh patterns that ground the content.

## 2. High-End Editorial Academy Aesthetic
- **Color Harmony:**
  - Background: Warm architectural ivory (`#faf8f5`, `#f5f3ed`) and deep academic midnight (`#090d16`).
  - Accents: Burnished gold (`#b45309`, `#d97706`), Oxford navy (`#02457a`), forest emerald (`#047857`).
- **Typographic Hierarchy:**
  - Display & Academy Titles: `Cinzel` serif / `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold.
  - Classical Citations & Recipient Names: `Cormorant Garamond` italic serif.
  - Technical Data & Credentials: `JetBrains Mono` for IDs, syllabi codes, and dates.
- **Tactile Materiality:**
  - Hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` or `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)`).
  - Subtle realistic shadows rather than excessive neon glows.
  - Authentic STS circular branding on certificates, seals, and credentials.
